About the University

MJP Rohilkhand University

MJP Rohilkhand University, Bareilly, is a state university offering undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across multiple disciplines — from engineering and management to sciences, humanities, and law. Spread across a green, walkable campus, it brings together students from across Uttar Pradesh and beyond under one academic roof.

The university's affiliated colleges extend its reach across the region, giving students access to labs, libraries, sports infrastructure, and a campus culture built around both rigorous study and everyday student life — the kind Campus Pulse exists to listen to.
